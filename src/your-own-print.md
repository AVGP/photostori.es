It works the way the box works, only the photograph is yours.

Martin photographs you, or something that matters to you: a person, a place, a room, a thing you have kept. Mostly in and around Zürich, where he is, and elsewhere when the trip makes sense.

From the session you choose the one picture you like most. Then you tell Eva what the back should carry: a moment, a person, a sentence you want kept. She writes it for that picture and nothing else.

You get the print, front and back. If you want it to live in the box here as well, that is your choice, not ours.

To ask about a session, write to hello@photostori.es with where you are and what the photograph is of. We will come back with dates and the price.

Photographs, Martin Splitt. The back, Eva Mermingi.
